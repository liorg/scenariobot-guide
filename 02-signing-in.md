# 02. Signing in

**How to get there:** open the site. If you're not signed in you land here
automatically.

## First screen

Signed out, you see the landing page with a **Log in** button. Pressing it opens
the sign-in form.

## Signing in

Email and password.

## New accounts

A new account confirms by email before first use. Check the inbox for the
confirmation link — until it's clicked, the password won't work.

## Forgotten password

**Reset password** is on the login screen. You'll get an email with a link; the
link opens a screen where you set a new one and are then signed in.

## Signing out

**Logout** is at the far end of the top bar, next to your avatar.

## Privacy policy

Linked in the footer of every page, and reachable at `/privacy` without signing
in.
